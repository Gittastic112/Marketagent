# Research and demo decisions

The [versioned register](../../data/reviews/research-demo-decisions.json) owns
individual decisions, evidence, rationale, and supersession. This page locates
those records and explains how they relate to the current project.

## Current scope

The historical company universe and eleven saved scenarios were removed on
November 5. November 9 authorized a four-company demo with hypothetical holdings
and Sample research. The [current-state record](../current-state.md) owns those
dated boundaries. Historical approvals do not recreate removed data or establish
current company facts, current valuations, or genuine portfolio transactions.

[The acceptance tracker](completion-tracker.md) owns milestone gates; the
[limitation ledger](../limitations.md) owns unresolved prerequisites.

## Review authority

AUTH-20261017-DELEGATED-REVIEW records the user's delegation for research/demo
financial, valuation, and interpretation-review choices within the stated scope.
The [original authority and readable history](../archive/assessments/research-demo-decisions-2026-11-04.md#review-authority)
remain available. A decision made under that delegation is agent review under
user delegation, distinct from personal user inspection and independent
reference certification. Source evidence and current permission boundaries
continue to apply.

## Revisit a choice

1. Identify the decision ID and inspect its exact evidence, scope, and input versions.
2. Record a new dated entry with supersedes_decision_id, rationale, sources,
   assumptions, affected records, and remaining uncertainty. Preserve the old entry.
3. Recheck current source/candidate versions and authorization before applying a
   material change. A decision record alone does not close an acceptance gate.
