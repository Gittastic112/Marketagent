The approval choice

Approve applying these named scenarios and their selected assumptions to the app after implementation checks pass, with retained-source receipts and explicitly unresolved inputs preserved. Approval does not certify missing company facts, establish five business days of actual use, authorize public deployment or grant GitHub authority through this report. This document records no approval and performs no application.

Unknown claims stay unknown. Several proposals use discretionary reserves, full plan capacity or fixed subsidiary ownership. These choices are not best estimates, probabilities, measured liabilities or proven upper/lower bounds. A zero stress endpoint does not assert an absent claim. Decimal precision in the calculations does not establish economic accuracy.

Shared method choices

The US cases preserve the dated growth paths, benchmark margin fade and reinvestment inputs. Their component discount rates use dated priors and chosen capital assumptions, not an observed current company financing mix. Parent awards included in the denominator receive no second monetary deduction. Operating lease expense and stock compensation remain in reported operating profit.

The bank cases use reported parent income and common book equity, a chosen equity discount rate, a fade in return on equity, and fixed subsidiary ownership. Parent options are counted once. The separate issuance and equity-haircut stresses must not be mechanically combined.

Reliance and Airtel use explicit direct INR discount rates, stationary revenue and margin, and dated balance-sheet proxies. Missing financing components do not become facts. Funding-component sensitivities and supplementary equity models remain unavailable where their own required inputs are absent. GOOG and GOOGL use the same issuer economics under a no-voting-premium assumption; their issuer values must not be added together.
