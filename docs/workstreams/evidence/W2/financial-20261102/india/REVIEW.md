The source amounts are historical facts/calculations. Adopting their carrying
amounts in an equity bridge is a **User Assumption**. In particular, reported cash
is not necessarily surplus cash and carrying NCI/investments are not current fair
values. Six calculation records and fourteen independent arithmetic checks are
retained; original source and extract hashes were checked.

Reliance's zero-debt draft must include the separate spectrum financing principal,
including its current amount inside other financial liabilities. Airtel already
includes spectrum/AGR deferred liabilities in borrowing, so adding them again
would double count. Leases remain separate in both. Reported margin replaces the
benchmark prior only for the historical current margin; no target margin or
forecast path is silently approved.

Airtel's investment proxy includes the separately presented associate/JV balance
that the generic current/noncurrent investment fields omit. Its cash-flow closing
balance includes unavailable mobile-money trust money and subtracts overdraft;
that value must not replace balance-sheet cash. The source tax reversals are not
pretax exceptional earnings adjustments. Airtel's INR48,729m NCI put liability
still needs a consistent treatment against NCI before a complete valuation bridge.

Both companies have outstanding parent/subsidiary awards. Zero award deductions
are rejected. Annual EPS-weighted shares also do not establish a current or
March 31 fully diluted denominator. Airtel's end-period issued fully-paid share
count alone exceeds its annual diluted EPS share count. Accordingly the packet
sets award value, diluted denominator and the different-date price comparison to
null and removes their review approvals. The source facts are preserved.

Zero parent preferred capital is supported by the issued/paid-up schedules. The
nonzero preference counts in both reports describe **authorized** capital, not
issued claims; Airtel's authorized amount also rounds to zero million. This does
not certify subsidiary financing/NCI obligations absent.

Reliance net external-revenue weights remain unavailable. The retained segment
table reconciles external gross turnover, GST and intersegment eliminations, but
does not explicitly allocate segment GST between external and internal sales.
Subtracting each GST row from external gross turnover is an unverified inference,
not an approved external-net mix. Keep the existing disclosed Diversified taxonomy
comparison. Airtel's previously reviewed external-customer mix stays unchanged;
its India benchmark is not a substitute for Africa country risk, and acquisition-
affected historical growth is not a reviewed organic forecast.
