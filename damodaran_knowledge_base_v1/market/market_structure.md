# Market Structure, Momentum and Technical Events

Classify market events as:
- fundamental
- valuation-risk
- capital-structure
- information/expectations
- technical/flow
- sentiment/momentum

Index inclusion/removal can affect visibility, liquidity, trading volume and short-term flows without necessarily changing intrinsic value. Historical inclusion effects may be transitory.

Momentum can amplify price moves in either direction. Do not convert a technical flow into a DCF assumption unless it changes the economics of cash flows or risk.

For each event, record separately:
- intrinsic-value impact
- pricing/multiple impact
- short-term flow impact
- sentiment impact
- catalyst horizon
