# SpaceX 2026 Case

Purpose: worked example of valuing a young, multi-business company with limited initial data and then revising the valuation when a prospectus arrives.

## Core lesson
The initial valuation is built from a business narrative despite limited financial history. The prospectus then causes two separate updates:
1. **data update**: replace estimated base-year numbers with reported numbers
2. **story update**: change target revenue, margins, reinvestment and risk only where new evidence changes the economics

## Segments
- Launch
- Starlink/connectivity
- xAI
- Expansion/other options

## Value-driver logic
- Launch: reusable rockets/cost advantage -> strong share and margins; slower market maturation can reduce near-term growth.
- Starlink: subscriber growth, ARPU, gross margin and scale matter separately.
- xAI: very large possible market, but intense competition, compute cost and capital intensity reduce margin/reinvestment economics.
- Expansion: low-probability/high-payoff opportunities should be treated as optionality or delayed scenario value, not automatic base revenue.

## Uncertainty
Uncertainty differs by segment. Use explicit assumption ranges/distributions rather than mechanically inflating WACC for all company-specific uncertainty.

## Pricing vs value
The case demonstrates why forward multiples and hand-picked peers can hide assumptions. Intrinsic valuation forces revenue, margin and reinvestment assumptions to be explicit.
